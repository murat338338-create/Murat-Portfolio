01AERØFinal-year project · Sport product, brand and launch · 10 weeks2026

AERØ

A shoe dryer you clip in after training.

A portable, battery-powered dryer for athletes, designed to cut moisture and odour wherever they train. I tested early concepts with target athletes before any CAD, then retested and refined the 3D-printed working prototype. One brand system runs across logo, packaging, retail display and a co-branded 10K run and recovery event.

02FreshaGroup project, team of up to four · Product and app · 6 weeks2026

Fresha

Eat confidently. Waste less.

A handheld scanner that reads spoilage gases and bacteria and shows, in seconds, whether food is still safe to eat. The companion app turns every meal saved into CO₂ saved, so cutting waste feels rewarding rather than a chore.

My part: I storyboarded the journey (unsure, scan, decide) and designed three app screens, including a Result screen that turns a raw sensor reading into one clear safety score.

Sketch development · CAD and render · UX and app · Storyboard

04PosturaInteractive prototype · Wearable tech · 4 weeks2024

Postura

A wristband that notices the slouch before you do.

An Arduino wearable with a classifier I trained in Edge Impulse on motion data. It learns the difference between good and poor posture, then vibrates to nudge you upright before slouching becomes a habit. No app needed.

SolidWorks · KeyShot · Procreate · Arduino · Edge Impulse

06AnatudeGroup project, team of up to four · Modular outdoor apparel—

Anatude

Layers that change with the mountain.

A modular clothing system that keeps climbers warm, mobile and comfortable as conditions change at altitude. Inspired by the Annapurna range and the Himalayan Monal, its shell, mid and base layers and zip-off vented panels let the wearer adjust protection through the climb.

08IndustryJun 2025 – Jun 2026

A year at Contex Textile and Conuk Ltd.

Product Design Development Intern in Istanbul. Contex is a textile sourcing and production partner for retailers and fashion brands, founded in 1997. It brings design, sourcing, production monitoring, quality assurance and export documents under one address, and works with evaluated factories to make each product. Conuk Ltd is its sister company in London.

  • Worked with suppliers and factories on samples and specifications, designing within real cost and deadline constraints.
  • Designed brand presentations for buyer meetings, keeping one visual identity across Contex and Conuk.
  • Redesigned the Conuk Ltd website in Figma. It’s now live (Chapter 03).
  1. BriefRange direction from the customer
  2. SourcingWorking with suppliers on fabric and trims
  3. SamplingFollowing samples between factory and team
  4. SpecsSupporting specifications for production
  5. PresentationBrand materials for buyer meetings

What it taught me: creative intent always meets cost, lead time and factory capability, and raising issues early saves a sample round. The ranges I worked on are confidential, so no product images are shown.

Chapter 06Murat Kurul

About

Whatever the medium, I start with the person using it.

I’m a London-based multidisciplinary designer, trained in industrial design at Loughborough. I work across product, brand, apparel and digital: a battery-powered shoe dryer, a food-safety scanner and its app, my own clothing label and two live websites. Research comes first: interviews, user testing and thematic analysis shape the flows before the screens.
