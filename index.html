04 / Modular outdoor apparel system

Anatude

A modular clothing system that keeps climbers warm, mobile and comfortable as conditions change at altitude. Inspired by the Annapurna range and the Himalayan Monal, its shell, mid and base layers and zip-off vented panels let the wearer adjust protection through the climb.

I built the moodboard around the Monal’s iridescent colour shifts and the textures of snow and rock, then translated them into gradient prints and blocked panels. Next I’d add technical flats, a fabric and trim list, and a toned-down colourway for a wider customer.

  • Layering system
  • Colour story
  • Line-up sketches

About

Murat Kurul

I design where sport, culture and everyday style meet.

I’m a London-based designer who trained in industrial design at Loughborough and spent a year in-house at Contex Textile in Istanbul, around garment sourcing, sampling and buyer presentations. I played BUCS basketball for two seasons and wrote my dissertation on how what players wear shapes their confidence and identity on court.
