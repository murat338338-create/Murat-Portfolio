06 / Modular outdoor apparel system · group projectTeam of up to 4

Anatude

A modular clothing system that keeps climbers warm, mobile and comfortable as conditions change at altitude. Inspired by the Annapurna range and the Himalayan Monal, its shell, mid and base layers and zip-off vented panels let the wearer adjust protection through the climb.

The moodboard follows the Monal’s iridescent colour shifts and the textures of snow and rock, translated into gradient prints and blocked panels.

07 / Consumer insight · dissertation

What players wear changes how they play.

About

Murat Kurul

Whatever the medium, I start with the person using it.

I’m a London-based multidisciplinary designer, trained in industrial design at Loughborough. I work across product, brand, apparel and digital: a battery-powered shoe dryer, a food-safety scanner and its app, my own clothing label and two live websites. A year in-house at Contex Textile in Istanbul showed me how ideas meet suppliers, cost and deadlines.
