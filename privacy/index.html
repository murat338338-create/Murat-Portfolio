Privacy notice

This is a personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. The only personal data involved is what any website needs to load, and anything you choose to send me.

Who I am

This site belongs to Murat Kurul, an individual designer based in London. I decide how the small amount of personal data described here is used. You can reach me at mmuratkurul@gmail.com.

What happens when you visit

I don’t collect anything about you directly, and the site doesn’t store anything on your device. Two services see your visit because the site can’t load without them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. GitHub decides how long it keeps these logs; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser sends Google your IP address, the page address and your browser type so it can deliver the fonts. Google says it sets no cookies and doesn’t use this to build profiles or target ads; see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Both companies may process this data outside the UK, under their own safeguards for international transfers. I rely on my legitimate interest in running a working website to use them.

If you contact me

If you email, call or message me on LinkedIn, I’ll use your name, contact details and what you send only to reply and to follow up on the opportunity we’re discussing. I won’t add you to a mailing list or share your details. I keep the conversation for as long as it’s relevant, and I’ll delete it sooner if you ask. Emails are held by my email provider, Google (Gmail).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask me for a copy of any personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email me and I’ll respond within one month. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Links to other sites

This site links to LinkedIn and to conukltd.co.uk. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this one.

Changes

If anything above changes, for example if I ever add analytics, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
